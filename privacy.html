Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Who we are

invoice-notifications, Bengaluru, India, is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: no-reply@alerts.invoice-notifications.com.

What we collect

Your email address, your name if you give it, and anything you include when you write to us. Our hosting provider processes basic technical logs (time of request, browser details) to run and protect the website.

Why we use it

To answer your questions, send updates you agreed to receive, respect unsubscribe requests, keep business records, and meet legal obligations. We rely on your consent for optional updates, and you can withdraw it at any time. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails: up to 24 months after our last meaningful contact, unless the law requires longer. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, we keep only a minimal record so that we never email you again.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask for a summary of the data we hold about you, its correction or completion, its erasure, and you may withdraw consent. Email no-reply@alerts.invoice-notifications.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We respond within 30 days.

Grievances

Grievance contact: NA, no-reply@alerts.invoice-notifications.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Sharing and security

We share limited data only with providers that run our email and hosting, and where the law requires it. We take reasonable steps to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy first.
