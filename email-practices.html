Email practices

Email should be expected, identifiable and easy to stop.

How we email and how you can manage your preferences.

Who receives our emails

People who contacted us, asked for updates, or agreed to hear from us. We do not send unsolicited bulk email and never use purchased, scraped or rented lists.

Recognising our messages

Our sender name is invoice-notifications, and our emails come from addresses on alerts.invoice-notifications.com. Links in our emails lead to invoice-notifications.com, never to link shorteners.

How to unsubscribe

Reply to any of our emails with the word unsubscribe, or email no-reply@alerts.invoice-notifications.com with “Unsubscribe” in the subject. We act on every request within 2 days.

After you unsubscribe we may still answer a message you send us, but we never use that to send optional updates.

Report a suspicious message

If an email claiming to be from us looks unusual, do not reply with passwords or financial details. Contact us using the details on this website, not the links in that email.
